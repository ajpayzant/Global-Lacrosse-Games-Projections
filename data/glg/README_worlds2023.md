# worlds_2023_rosters.csv

Rosters for the 2023 World Lacrosse Men's Championship (San Diego, June 21 - July 1 2023).
Columns: nation, first_name, last_name, position, source_url. Compiled 2026-11-24.

## USA (23)
- Primary: USA Lacrosse, "USA Lacrosse Names Final Roster for 2023 World Lacrosse Men's Championship" (Dec 20, 2022):
  https://www.usalacrosse.com/news-media-blog/usa-lacrosse-names-final-roster-2023-world-lacrosse-mens-championship
- Cross-check: Wikipedia "United States men's national lacrosse team" 2023 roster. All 23 names and positions agree.
- World Lacrosse awards and stats pages also name O'Neill, Sowers, Giles-Harris, Riorden, Pannell, Baptiste and Ierlan as USA.
  The stats page spells Riorden's first name "Blake". The official roster says "Blaze", which is correct.

## Canada (23)
- Source: Wikipedia "Canada men's national lacrosse team". It cites Lacrosse Canada articles
  lacrosse.ca/article/85457 (roster, June 9 2023) and lacrosse.ca/article/85783 (change, June 20 2023).
  Both now return 404: Lacrosse Canada rebuilt its site and the 2023 posts were not carried over.
- Roster change: Owen Grant (D) was named but withdrew injured. Michael Grace replaced him on June 20 2023.
  The CSV has the final 23 (Grace in, Grant out).
- Partial cross-check: World Lacrosse match reports, awards and stats pages name Teat, Byrne, Hossack,
  Dhane Smith, Petterson and Fields as Canada, which is consistent. No second full list could be reached.

## Australia (23)
- Source: Wikipedia "Australia men's national lacrosse team" 2023 roster. It cites Lacrosse Australia,
  "Announcement - 2023 Australian Men's Team" (Jan 23 2023):
  https://lacrosse.com.au/news/2023/01/23/4rmzg0ibe52q9x6hdt3mjnrd2iyebu-gb2g6/
  That page is still live, but the fetched text has no player names, so the list is probably an image.
  The names could not be read from the primary source.
- Not in the CSV: four alternates listed by Wikipedia, namely Jaesaya Bidwell-Barton, Sean Clarke,
  Patrick Palmer and Jack Price. It is unknown whether any of them replaced a named player before the tournament.
- Partial cross-check: the World Lacrosse stats page names Sean Aaron (Australia).
- Positions use Australian spelling as on the source ("Defence", "Faceoff"). Wikipedia's "MIdfield" typo
  for Parsons Quintiao is normalised to Midfield.

## Haudenosaunee: NOT INCLUDED
I could not find a full roster I could verify. The Haudenosaunee Nationals site (Wix blog) only goes back
to about mid-2024. The Wikipedia team page has no 2023 roster. The tournament stats site (worldlax2023.com)
no longer resolves. Inside Lacrosse and web.archive.org were blocked, and the search engines available
returned nothing relevant. The only players confirmed on the 2023 team (World Lacrosse reports) are
Lyle Thompson, Austin Staats, Randy Staats, Brendan Bomberry and Jacob Piseno. That is not a full roster,
so the nation is left out.
